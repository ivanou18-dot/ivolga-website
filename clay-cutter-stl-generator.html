Frequently Asked Clay Cutter CAD Questions

Does Pixel2Path Studio ship physical clay cutters or generate digital .STL files?
Pixel2Path Studio is 100% browser-based digital CAD software. You download instant 1:1 millimeter .STL, .SVG, and .DXF files to slice and print on your own 3D printer (such as Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, or Anycubic). No physical cutters, stamps, or tools are manufactured or shipped.
How is the 0.42mm stepped polymer clay micro-cutter blade engineered for 0.4mm FDM nozzles?
Instead of a fragile single-wall extrusion that snaps or disappears in standard slicers, Pixel2Path Studio generates a 3-tier stepped cross-section totaling 10.5 mm in Z height: a 3.2 mm wide top finger-grip flange, a rigid 1.2 mm structural mid-wall, and a tapered 0.42 mm top cutting lip calibrated specifically for single-perimeter Arachne wall generation on a standard 0.4 mm nozzle.
How does the 2-Part Cutter + Stamp Pair .STL maintain registration without binding in polymer clay?
Selecting 🧁 2-Part Cutter + Stamp Pair in Step 2B exports both pieces side-by-side in one watertight 1:1 mm .STL file: the outer stepped 0.42 mm Contour Offset cutter and a matched inner raised-relief embossing stamp inset by an exact 0.70 mm radial clearance gap so you can press crisp details without dragging clay edges.
What Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, or PrusaSlicer settings should I use for 0.42mm clay cutters?
Import the .STL in millimeters (1:1 scale), set Quality → Wall Generator → Arachne, slice at 0.16 mm layer height with a 0.4 mm (or 0.2 mm) nozzle, use 4–5 top/bottom shells, 25%–40% gyroid/grid infill for cutters (80%–100% for heavy press stamps), and disable supports—every cutter flange, stamp baseplate, and pottery chop grip prints flat on the bed.
