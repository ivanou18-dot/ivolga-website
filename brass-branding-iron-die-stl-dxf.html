Frequently Asked CNC Brass Die & Branding Iron Questions

Does Pixel2Path Studio machine physical brass branding irons or generate digital .DXF and .STL CAD files?

No physical brass dies, heating irons, or arbor presses are sold or shipped.
How are the M5 x 0.8 and M8 x 1.25 rear blind tap pilot bores sized and protected on narrow plaques?
Pixel2Path bores exact ISO metric tap-drill pilot cylinders into the Z = 0 rear face of the 8.0 mm baseplate: Ø 4.20 mm (radius 2.10 mm) for M5 × 0.8 soldering/arbor shanks and Ø 6.80 mm (radius 3.40 mm) for heavy-duty M8 × 1.25 electric branding irons, capped at ≤ min(6.0 mm, Z_base − 1.8 mm) blind depth so threads never pierce the relief face. Critically, the engine validates ≥ 1.5 mm concave side-wall clearance around the true un-shrunk pilot radius—automatically relocating the bore to a safe interior centroid on narrow shapes or cleanly skipping boring rather than ever shrinking hole diameter and snapping your tap.
Should I import the AutoCAD R2010 .DXF or the 3D .STL into Fusion 360, Vectric VCarve, or Carveco?
Both are included on every pass. For flat-topped maker's marks, leather stamps, and wood branding irons, importing the AutoCAD R2010 .DXF ($INSUNITS = 4 mm) is typically 5× faster to machine: pocket the space between BASEPLATE_CUT and RAISED_RELIEF using a 3.175 mm (1/8") flat end mill for bulk clearance plus a 20°–30° V-bit rest pass. Use the watertight 8.0 mm .STL when machining 12° stepped draft dies, 2-sided rear M5/M8 pilot setups, or variable-Z 3D Machined Coins and AI Studio Clay bas-reliefs.
Why do 12-degree draft sidewalls and >=0.38mm stroke guards matter for leather and hot-foil brass dies?
Under 1-ton arbor-press tonnage or 120–300 °C heated wood/foil branding, 90° vertical micro-letters bend, trap scorched fibers, or snap fragile 0.25 mm end mills during roughing. Conditioning fine strokes to ≥ 0.38 mm (≥ 0.44 mm grooves) and stepping sidewalls at a 12° pyramidal draft gives delicate serifs a wide structural root fused into the 8.0 mm C36000 brass baseplate while allowing clean, non-binding release from vegetable-tanned leather and hot-foil stock.
