🎨 Step 1: Choose What to Create (Image, Text, or Both)

Start by selecting one of the three creation modes from the gold dropdown menu at the top of the workspace:

🖼️ 1. Logo / Photo Mode

Best For: Makers mark stamps, multi-color emblems, smartphone photos, 3D sculptures, and physical part templates.

  • Multi-Color & B&W Logos: Our Proprietary Optical Relief & Multi-Channel Vector Engine automatically separates touching colors (such as a yellow body touching dark wings) with a clean separation gap and preserves internal feather slits.
  • Photos & Busy Backgrounds: Automatically pre-cleaned on upload by Advanced Vision into crisp B&W stamp line-art (or tap ✨ Advanced Vision Removes Background in the readiness panel anytime).
  • Revert Anytime: Tap ↩ Revert to Original if you want to switch back to your raw uncleaned upload.

✍️ 2. Custom Text Mode

Best For: Maker touchmarks, leather word stamps, cookie/soap/clay text stamps, laser stencils, and clicker-die patches.

  • 7 Production-Tested Fonts: Choose from Clean Bold Block, Classic Serif, Heavy Impact Block, Military / Industrial Stencil, Vintage Typewriter, Varsity / Athletic Block, or Cursive / Handwritten Script.
  • Adjustable Stroke Weight: Open 🎛️ Optional Fine-Tuning to adjust Letter Boldness (1–10) (`1–2` for fine laser/resin, `3–5` for 0.4mm FDM 3D stamps, `6–10` for heavy embossing).

🔀 3. Logo + Text Combo Mode

Best For: Custom seals, arched brand stamps, shop badges, and medallions combining an icon with custom text.

  • Text Below Logo: Stacks your custom text centered underneath your graphic.
  • Text Above Logo: Stacks your custom text centered above your graphic.
  • Text Arced Around Logo: Automatically calculates your logo’s outer radius and curves your text smoothly around the top arc without letter collisions. Adjust Text Size (% of Logo) from `50%` to `150%`.

📜 Using the 1-Click Advanced Vision Prompt Studio

Want to generate a brand-new graphic or pre-clean a tricky photo inside ChatGPT or Google Gemini? Click the 📜 1-Click Advanced Vision Prompt Studio expander above the file uploader:

  1. Select Target Machine Style: Choose Strict-Fidelity 3D Stamp & CNC Cleanup, Classic 3D Banknote & Intaglio Engraving, Vinyl Plotter & Drag-Knife Stencil, or Clicker Die Outer Silhouette Only.
  2. Enter Your Subject / Photo Description: Type what you want to create or describe your photo (for example: Galloping horse or your custom brand text).
  3. Tap 🔄 Enter / Update Prompt Text: Instantly locks your custom description into the engineered prompt template.
  4. Tap 📋 1-Click Copy Prompt to Clipboard: Copies the complete prompt to your clipboard so you can paste it directly into ChatGPT or Gemini alongside your photo.

📐 Slicer & CAD Import Cheat-Sheet (1:1 Millimeter Scale)

Every file exported by ToolPath Studio is pre-calibrated in exact 1:1 Millimeters (mm). When importing into your software:

  • Bambu Studio / PrusaSlicer / Cura / OrcaSlicer (.STL): Import the .STL directly (defaults to millimeters). Set Wall Generator to Arachne, layer height to 0.16mm – 0.20mm, and top shell layers to 4–5. Check 🔄 Mirror Horizontally in ToolPath Studio before exporting if making a face-down ink, wax, leather, or clay stamp.
  • LightBurn / xTool / Glowforge (.SVG or .DXF): Import with units set to Millimeters (mm). Red paths represent inner/relief vector loops; black paths (when applicable) represent the outer border cut.
  • Silhouette Studio / Cricut Design Space (.SVG or .DXF): Import the .SVG file. If your logo has ultra-thin lines that lift during weeding, open 🎛️ Optional Fine-Tuning in ToolPath Studio and set Extra Line Thickness Boost to +1 or +2.
  • Fusion 360 / AutoCAD / SolidWorks / Vectric VCarve (.DXF): Set import units to Millimeters (mm). All paths are exported as clean, closed R2010 LWPOLYLINE loops separated onto named layers (STEEL_RULE_CUT, RAISED_RELIEF, or BASEPLATE_CUT).
