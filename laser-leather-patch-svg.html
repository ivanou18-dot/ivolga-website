Frequently Asked Laser Patch & DXF Questions

Does Pixel2Path Studio sell physical leather patches or digital .SVG and .DXF files?

It generates instant 1:1 millimeter .SVG, .DXF, and .STL files for you to engrave and cut on your own CO2/diode laser (xTool, Glowforge, OMTech, Thunder, Epilog), clicker press, or CNC machine. No physical patches, stamps, or dies are sold or shipped.
How are color layers and DXF layer names structured for LightBurn and die-board CAM?
In .SVG exports (1:1 mm viewBox, 0.0882 mm / 0.25 pt hairline), #FF0000 contains the unified fill-rule="evenodd" Cubic Bezier engraving compound path (inner letter counters pre-grouped so holes never engrave solid), #0055FF contains the 1.1 mm perimeter stitch holes, #000000 contains the outer patch or keychain cutline, and #00A859 contains the optional 50.0 × 50.0 mm "50mm REF" calibration square. In AutoCAD R2010 .DXF exports ($INSUNITS = 4 mm, 0.09 mm hairline), closed LWPOLYLINE loops are separated onto LASER_OR_CUT_PATHS, STITCH_HOLES, OUTER_BORDER_CUT, STEEL_RULE_CUT, and SCALE_REF_50MM.
How do I verify true 1:1 millimeter import scale in LightBurn or Adobe Illustrator?
Enable 📏 Include 50×50 mm Scale Calibration Box (.SVG / .DXF) before exporting. When you import the .SVG or .DXF into LightBurn, Illustrator, CorelDRAW, or AutoCAD at 1 Unit = 1 Millimeter, select the green #00A859 (SCALE_REF_50MM) square and confirm its dimensions read exact 50.00 × 50.00 mm—eliminating 72/96 DPI scaling errors before firing your laser—then hide or delete that reference box.
What is the difference between Laser Stitch-Hole Patches and Clicker Die Stitch Punches?
Laser Engraver Stitch-Hole Leather Hat Patch mode generates 1.1 mm diameter stitch holes spaced at 3.8 mm pitch calibrated for 0.10 mm laser kerf alongside your interior engraving vectors. Clicker Die Leather Patch mode generates 1.2 mm tube-punch circles on STITCH_HOLES paired with bend-safe 2 pt (0.71 mm) blade geometry on STEEL_RULE_CUT (and locks artwork dilation to 0.0 mm when a Step 2B frame is active so die boards never receive duplicate cut lines).
