6. Slicer, Laser & CAD Import Guidelines

🧊 Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer & Cura (.STL) • Import units: Millimeters (mm); enable Quality ➔ Wall Generator ➔ Arachne.
• 🔼 Raised Stamps, 🔪 Clay Micro-Cutters & 🔽 Wax Seals: 0.16–0.20 mm layers (0.16 mm for 0.42 mm clay cutter lips), 4–5 top shells, and 80%–100% solid infill for arbor/clicker leather presses.
• 🔩 M5 / M8 Threaded Brass Dies (CNC / Metal Print): Rear blind pilot bore is pre-modeled at Ø 4.20 mm (tap with standard M5 × 0.8 bottoming tap) or Ø 6.80 mm (tap with M8 × 1.25 bottoming tap).
• 🗿 Sculpted 3D Bas-Relief Plaques & Busts: Slice at 0.12–0.16 mm layer height with 4–5 top shells; switch slicer preview Color Scheme ➔ Height and tilt 35° to inspect full Z-relief depth.
📐 Cubic Bezier .SVG vs. R2010 .DXF in CAD, Cricut & LightBurn • Set import scale to 1 Unit = 1 Millimeter (mm).
• .SVG (Default for Laser & Vinyl Plotter): Exports tangent-continuous Cubic Bezier curves (capped at ≤4,800 subpaths / ≤12,000 nodes for instant Cricut & LightBurn loading) with exact 0.0882mm (0.25pt) hairlines: #FF0000 (unified fill-rule="evenodd" artwork compound path), #000000 (outer border / keychain tab), #0055FF (stitch holes), and #00A859 (50mm ref box).
• .DXF (Default for Clicker Die & CNC): Exports closed AutoCAD R2010 LWPOLYLINE loops (lineweight=9, 0.09 mm / 0.25 pt hairline) on dedicated layers: LASER_OR_CUT_PATHS, OUTER_BORDER_CUT, STITCH_HOLES, STEEL_RULE_CUT, RAISED_RELIEF, BASEPLATE_CUT, and SCALE_REF_50MM (ideal for AutoCAD, Fusion 360, Vectric VCarve, and free Silhouette Studio Basic Edition). To group inner holes manually: Illustrator Ctrl+8 • Silhouette Ctrl+E • LightBurn Alt+J.
