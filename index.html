How do I convert text, a logo, or arched text + logo to .SVG or .DXF for laser engraving?
In Pixel2Path Studio, choose 🖼️ Logo / Photo, ✍️ Custom Text, or 🔀 Logo + Text Combo (straight or concentrically arched), then select the ⚡ Laser Engraver preset. Enter your target millimeter width and export a unified Compound Path .SVG (with an exact 0.0882mm / 0.25pt hairline stroke and non-scaling display strokes) or 1:1 mm .DXF for LightBurn, Glowforge, xTool, and laser rubber stamps.
Should I choose .SVG or .DXF for Laser Engravers, Clicker Dies, CNC, and Vinyl Plotters?
Both formats are included. .SVG is the default for Laser Engravers and Vinyl Plotters (Cricut Design Space, Adobe Illustrator, LightBurn, Glowforge)—it exports as a single unified <Compound Path> with inner holes pre-grouped, an exact 0.0882mm (0.25pt) hairline stroke, and crisp browser visibility (vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"). .DXF is the default for Clicker Dies and CNC / Brass Dies (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Vectric VCarve, die-board lasers) and for free Silhouette Studio Basic Edition. Note: .DXF and .SVG files use mathematical hairlines (0.25 pt / 0.0882 mm); zoom in or enable Outline / Hairline view in CAD software if lines appear faint.
What uses Advanced Vision Credits vs. Unlimited CAD Exports?
All standard logo tracing, pre-drawn intaglio tracing, custom arched text, classical banknote hatching sliders, and 1:1 millimeter .STL, .DXF, and .SVG downloads use 0 credits and are 100% unlimited. Only using Advanced Vision to strip complex photo backgrounds or transform a flat logo into a Banknote Intaglio engraving uses 1 credit once per uploaded image (toggling between styles or clearing and restoring the cached enhancement afterwards costs 0 extra credits).
