6. Slicer, Laser & CAD Import Guidelines

🧊 Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer & Cura (.STL) • Import units: Millimeters (mm).
• Enable Arachne Wall Generator for sharp tapered details.
• Hand Stamps (Ink/Clay): 0.16–0.20 mm layers, 4 top shells, 25%–40% Gyroid/Grid infill.
• Arbor / Clicker Press Leather Stamps: 5+ top/bottom shells, 4–6 wall loops, 80%–100% solid infill.
• 💡 Need a Recessed Negative Mold (Soap/Wax/Clay)? Export the standard positive .STL. In Bambu Studio / PrusaSlicer, right-click the bed ➔ Add Primitive (Cube/Cylinder), right-click that shape ➔ Add Negative Part ➔ Load .STL to cut out a custom mold cavity in 2 clicks.
📐 .SVG vs. .DXF in Illustrator, Cricut, Silhouette & LightBurn • Set import scale to 1 Unit = 1 Millimeter (mm).
• .SVG (Default for Laser & Vinyl Plotter): Exports a single unified <Compound Path> with inner holes pre-grouped (fill-rule="evenodd") in a 1:1 millimeter viewBox with an exact 0.0882mm (0.25pt) mathematical hairline stroke ready for Illustrator, Cricut Design Space, Glowforge, and LightBurn.
• .DXF (Default for Clicker Die & CNC): Exports closed AutoCAD R2010 LWPOLYLINE loops (lineweight=9, 0.09 mm / 0.25 pt hairline) on dedicated layers (LASER_OR_CUT_PATHS, STEEL_RULE_CUT, RAISED_RELIEF, BASEPLATE_CUT, SCALE_REF_50MM) for AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Vectric VCarve, die-board lasers, and free Silhouette Studio Basic Edition. If a .DXF looks blank on open, zoom in or enable Outline / Hairline view. To group inner holes as a compound path manually:
  – Illustrator: Select All (Ctrl+A) ➔ Ctrl+8 (Cmd+8 on Mac)
  – Silhouette Studio: Select All ➔ Right-Click ➔ Make Compound Path (Ctrl+E)
  – LightBurn: Select All ➔ Press Alt+J (Auto-Join)
