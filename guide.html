4. Tracing Styles & AI Photo Pre-Conditioning

  • ⚡ Smart Auto-Trace (Recommended): Separates dark structural ink outlines from colored fills using a 3-pixel separation halo so adjacent colors never fuse into a solid blob. Preserves internal feather slits and extracts 3D facial contours via Multi-Scale Retinex, SAR Lee Despeckling, and Hessian Principal Curvature (λ₁).
  • 🏛️ Classical Banknote Hatching (3D Shading Lines): Converts smooth continuous grayscale reliefs and medallions into variable-width engraving lines (Diagonal 45°, Cross-Hatch 45° Mesh, Concentric Seal, Horizontal, Vertical) with adjustable 3D Contour Wave Curvature (0–10), or traces pre-drawn intaglio engravings directly.
  • ✨ Automatic & 1-Click Vision AI Cleanup: Strips background clutter, monitor borders, outer rims, and marble/stone speckles to pure white (#FFFFFF) while rendering the subject in crisp, connected black (#000000) stamp line-art.

5. Step-by-Step Operating Checklist

  1. Select Mode & Upload: Choose Logo / Photo, Custom Text, or Logo + Text Combo and upload a high-contrast PNG, JPG, or WEBP image (at least 500 × 500 px).
  2. Choose Workshop Machine & Tracing Style: Click your hardware preset and select ⚡ Smart Auto-Trace or 🏛️ Classical Banknote Hatching.
  3. Set Width (mm) & Orientation: Enter your exact target width in millimeters (5–350 mm). Check 🔄 Mirror Horizontally if making a face-down stamp (leather, ink, clay, brass die) or HTV iron-on transfer.
  4. Optional Fine-Tuning: Expand 🎛️ Optional Fine-Tuning only if you wish to customize 3D Base Thickness (0–15 mm), 3D Relief Height (0.5–15 mm), Detail Balance (-80 to +80), or Extra Line Thickness Boost (0–8).
  5. Verify & Export: Confirm the status bar shows ✅ Vector-Ready. Click 🧪 Download Free 25mm Test Sample to verify scaling in your slicer/CAD software for free, or enter your Access Key to click ⚡ Generate Full Production File.

6. Slicer, Laser & CAD Import Guidelines

🖨️ Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer & Cura (.STL) • Import units: Millimeters (mm).
• Enable Arachne Wall Generator for sharp tapered details.
• Hand Stamps (Ink/Clay): 0.16–0.20 mm layers, 4 top shells, 25%–40% Gyroid/Grid infill.
• Arbor / Clicker Press Leather Stamps: 5+ top/bottom shells, 4–6 wall loops, 80%–100% infill.
📐 Illustrator, AutoCAD & LightBurn (.DXF / .SVG) • Set import scale to 1 Unit = 1 Millimeter (mm).
• Illustrator Outline Tip: Press Ctrl + Y (Outline Mode) to inspect the zero-thickness mathematical cut path without Illustrator's default 0.25 mm visual stroke.
• LightBurn Rubber Stamps: Enable Ramp (0.5–1.0 mm) on the engraved fill layer.
