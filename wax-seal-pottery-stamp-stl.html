Why should I choose Recessed Cavity Seal instead of a standard Raised Stamp for sealing wax?
Traditional sealing wax impressions showcase raised positive lettering surrounded by a smooth recessed coin rim. A standard raised ink stamp pushes molten wax downward into a hollow trench, whereas Pixel2Path's 🔽 Recessed Cavity Seal mode carves an auto-mirrored intaglio pocket (≥ 1.5 mm cavity depth backed by an un-pierced ≥ 1.5 mm solid floor guard) inside a smooth rim so cooled wax releases as a crisp raised monogram.
Does the 20-26mm Handled Pottery Chop print in one piece without slicer supports?
Yes. 🕹️ Handled Stamp / Pottery Chop extrudes a solid 20–26 mm ergonomic finger grip directly from the Z = 0 build plate up to your face-up mirrored ceramic die face using support-free self-bridging transitions. You print the entire stamp + handle upside-down (grip on bed, crisp top layers forming the stamp face) in one rigid piece with zero support scarring on your maker's mark.
Which filament, nozzle size, and release prep work best for 3D printed wax seals and leather-hard stoneware?
For leather-hard stoneware, porcelain, and soap stamps, PLA or PETG on a 0.4 mm nozzle (0.16 mm layer height, Arachne wall generator, 80%–100% infill) works cleanly down to 18–25 mm marks; switch to a 0.2 mm FDM nozzle or SLA resin for micro 10–15 mm foot-ring chops and dust clay lightly with cornstarch. For hot sealing wax, print in PETG, ASA, or high-temp resin, chill the stamp face on an ice pack + wipe a trace of mineral oil before pressing into cooling Spoon/Gun wax.
