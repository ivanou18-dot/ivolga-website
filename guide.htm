1. The 4-Step Workflow (Zero CAD Cleanup Required)

Step 1: Choose Mode & Upload Artwork Select Logo / Photo, Custom Text, or Logo + Text Combo. Upload any PNG, JPG, or WEBP graphic on a clean white/transparent background (or use the in-app 1-Click AI Prompt Studio).
Step 2: Tap Your Workshop Machine Select 🖨️ 3D Printer, 🎯 Laser Engraver, ✂️ Vinyl Plotter, 🔪 Clicker Die, or ⚙️ CNC / Brass Die. ToolPath Studio automatically calibrates wall guards and weeding filters.
Step 3: Set Exact Target Width (mm) Enter your desired graphic width in millimeters (10 mm – 350 mm). Check Mirror Horizontally if making a face-down leather/ink stamp or HTV iron-on transfer.
Step 4: Test Free (25mm) or Export Full 1:1 Scale Click 🧪 Download Free 25mm Test Sample to verify scaling in your slicer/CAD software for free, or enter your Access Key ($8 24-Hr Pass or $25/mo Studio Monthly) to export full-scale production files.

3. Slicer & CAD Import Checklist

• Import Units: Always set your CAD, Laser, or Plotter import units to Millimeters (mm).
• 3D Slicer Settings (Bambu / Prusa / Cura): Use a 0.4mm or 0.2mm nozzle, enable Arachne Wall Generator, and use 4+ Top Shell Layers with 25%–40% Gyroid/Grid Infill (use 80%+ Infill for heavy arbor/clicker press leather embossing stamps).
