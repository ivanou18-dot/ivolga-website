How do I convert text, a logo, or arched text + logo to .SVG, .DXF, or a custom-shaped 3D stamp?
In Pixel2Path Studio, choose 🖼️ Logo / Photo (PNG, JPG, WEBP, SVG, TIFF, BMP, GIF, or HEIC — or load the built-in 🐦 Golden Oriole sample), ✍️ Custom Text, or 🔀 Logo + Text Combo (straight or concentrically arched). Select your machine in Step 2, pick your Tool Function and any of the 17 Boutique Stamp / Cut Silhouettes (such as Circle, Shield, Postage Stamp, Scalloped, Contour Offset, Stitch-Hole Hat Patch, Keychain Tab, or Polymer Clay Micro-Cutter) in Step 2B, set your millimeter width, and export a 1:1 mm .STL, Cubic Bezier Compound Path .SVG (0.0882mm / 0.25pt hairline), or R2010 .DXF (0.09mm hairline).
Should I choose .SVG or .DXF for Laser Engravers, Clicker Dies, CNC, and Vinyl Plotters?
Both formats are included. .SVG is the default for Laser Engravers and Vinyl Plotters (Cricut Design Space, Adobe Illustrator, LightBurn, Glowforge)—it exports tangent-continuous Cubic Bezier curves as a unified <Compound Path> with inner holes pre-grouped (fill-rule="evenodd") in a 1:1 millimeter viewBox with an exact 0.0882mm (0.25pt) hairline stroke, plus dedicated paths for outer borders and stitch holes. .DXF is the default for Clicker Dies and CNC / Brass Dies (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Vectric VCarve, die-board lasers) and for free Silhouette Studio Basic Edition, exporting closed R2010 LWPOLYLINE loops (lineweight=9 / 0.09 mm hairline) across dedicated layers (LASER_OR_CUT_PATHS, OUTER_BORDER_CUT, STITCH_HOLES, STEEL_RULE_CUT, RAISED_RELIEF, BASEPLATE_CUT).
