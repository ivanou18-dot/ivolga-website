Maker & Machinist Technical FAQ

Direct answers on converting logos, photos, and text into 3D stamps, clicker dies, and laser/CNC toolpaths.

How do I convert an image or text into a 3D printable leather stamp (.STL)?
Upload your PNG/JPG logo or type custom text into ToolPath Studio (3D Printer Mode) and enter your target millimeter width (3.0mm baseplate, 2.5mm relief height, horizontal mirroring enabled). The engine strips background noise, applies a 0.42mm minimum wall guard so thin lines slice cleanly in Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, or Cura, and exports a watertight binary .STL mesh.
How do I generate a steel-rule clicker die DXF cut line without internal holes?
Select the 🔪 Clicker Die preset. Unlike generic vectorizers that trace both inside and outside edges, Clicker Die mode strips all internal logo islands, suppresses table drop-shadows on photographed templates, smooths pixel jitter into sweeping arcs, and exports a single continuous outer perimeter on the STEEL_RULE_CUT layer in AutoCAD R2010 .DXF format.
Can I convert an image or text to a laser rubber stamp or CNC brass stamp?
Yes. For image or text to rubber stamp laser engraving, select 🎯 Laser Engraver to export closed-loop .SVG or .DXF vectors ready for LightBurn. For image or text to brass stamp machining, select ⚙️ CNC / Brass Die to export either a heavy 8.0mm base / 3.0mm relief 3D .STL solid or 2D V-carve toolpath vectors for Fusion 360 and Vectric.
How does Classical Banknote Line Engraving work on shaded photos and 3D reliefs?
Classical Line Engraving mode converts continuous color or grayscale shading into variable-width engraving lines with adjustable 3D contour wave curvature. You can choose Diagonal (45°) woodcut hatching, Cross-Hatch (45° Mesh) for deep currency-grade shadows, or Concentric Circles (Seal) for round medallions and wax seals.
Why are exported DXF, SVG, and STL files guaranteed to be exact 1:1 millimeter scale?
ToolPath Studio calculates the exact millimeter bounding box after all dust weeding, morphological smoothing, and polygon node approximation are complete. If you enter 60.0 mm, your exported part measures 60.00 mm in AutoCAD, Illustrator, LightBurn, and Bambu Studio with zero edge drift.
