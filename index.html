Should I choose .SVG or .DXF for Laser Engravers, Clicker Dies, CNC, and Vinyl Plotters?
Both formats are included. .SVG is the default for Laser Engravers and Vinyl Plotters (Cricut Design Space, Adobe Illustrator, LightBurn, Glowforge)—it exports tangent-continuous Cubic Bezier curves as a unified <Compound Path> with inner holes pre-grouped (fill-rule="evenodd") in a 1:1 millimeter viewBox with an exact 0.0882mm (0.25pt) hairline stroke, plus dedicated paths for outer borders and stitch holes. .DXF is the default for Clicker Dies and CNC / Brass Dies (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Vectric VCarve, die-board lasers) and for free Silhouette Studio Basic Edition, exporting closed R2010 LWPOLYLINE loops (lineweight=9 / 0.09 mm hairline) across dedicated layers (LASER_OR_CUT_PATHS, OUTER_BORDER_CUT, STITCH_HOLES, STEEL_RULE_CUT, RAISED_RELIEF, BASEPLATE_CUT).

Closed-Loop Compound Paths

Enforces 100% closed 'Z'/'z' fill-rule="evenodd" Cubic Bezier subpaths in .SVG and closed AutoCAD R2010 LWPOLYLINE bitflags (group 70 & 1 == 1) in .DXF, capped at ≤ 4,800 subpaths and ≤ 12,000 spline-faired nodes (0 open paths).

02

Duplicate STEEL_RULE_CUT Lock

Automatically locks clicker_offset_mm = 0.0 whenever any Step 2B outer Clicker Die silhouette is active and unifies compound cut borders (#000000 / OUTER_BORDER_CUT) so diemakers and lasers never receive overlapping double-cut loops.

03

1:1 mm Post-Filter Lock (±0.05 mm)

Computes 1 SVG unit = 1.000 mm ($INSUNITS = 4 in .DXF) strictly after morphological stroke conditioning and dilation, with zero-shrinkage fairing and clamped Bezier handles holding outer bounds within ±0.05 mm + optional 50.0 × 50.0 mm calibration square.

04

Sanitizes XML declarations (0 external <!DOCTYPE / <!ENTITY XXE vectors), rejects or traces embedded <image> bitmaps instead of wrapping pixels inside fake SVGs, and guarantees 0 unconverted live <text> font dependencies on cut layers.

05

Watertight 3F = 2E 2-Manifold .STL

Builds closed, consistently oriented 2-manifold triangle meshes satisfying 3F = 2E (0 boundary edges, 0 non-manifold edges, 0 collapsed vertex faces, 0.02 mm relief-to-baseplate Z-overlap) for zero-repair slicing in Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer & Cura.
